## Hi there 👋

I'm Lazar Atanackovic, leading the **AI ⋅ Cell Systems Lab** at the [University of Alberta](https://lazaratan.github.io/). 

### 🔬 About Me

I'm a researcher focused on developing cutting-edge machine learning methods for understanding and modeling natural (physical) systems from data, with an emphasis on cellular and molecular systems. My research combines theoretical advances with practical applications at the intersection of:

- 🧬 **Systems Biology** & Single-cell Biology
- 🤖 **Machine Learning** & Generative Modeling
- 🔗 **Causal Discovery** & Gene Regulatory Networks
- 💊 **Protein Design** & Drug Discovery
- 🌊 **Generative Flow Networks (GFlowNets)** & Diffusion Models
